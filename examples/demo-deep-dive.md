# Example: company deep dive on DEMO data

**This example uses invented demo numbers to show the format. It is not a
real company, not a real study, and not investment advice.**

## Thesis (demo)
Demo Co sells a single product to a stable market. Its edge is cost, not
growth — so the research question is whether its margins survive, not
whether it doubles.

## Financials (DEMO)
- Revenue: 1,000 | Gross profit: 400 | Operating profit: 150 | Net income: 90
- Gross margin 40%, operating margin 15%, net margin 9% (see toolkit ratios).

## Forensic (demo)
- Cash vs profit: not shown in demo data — listed as an open question, not assumed.
- Verdict: Watch, because the demo has no cash-flow data to check against.

## Valuation (demo)
- DCF on demo FCFs [100, 110, 121], discount 10%, terminal growth 3%,
  terminal-year FCF 130 → value 1,668.03 (see toolkit demo).
- Sanity check: at that value, what growth must be true? Answer in a real
  study, with real inputs — never from demo data.

## Open questions
- All of them. Demo data answers nothing about a real company, by design.
