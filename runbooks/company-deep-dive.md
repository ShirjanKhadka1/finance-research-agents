# Runbook: Company deep dive

**Team, in order:** Investment Researcher → Forensic Analyst → Financial
Analyst → Valuation Analyst → Reality Checker

**Time:** a real deep dive takes hours, not minutes. Do not rush a step to
save time — a skipped forensic pass is how a bad thesis survives.

## Step 1 — Investment Researcher
- Clarify company + exchange + period + decision the research is for.
- Thesis in one paragraph, business, industry.
- Output: initiation template, sections 1–3 filled, rest empty.

## Step 2 — Forensic Analyst
- Run the forensic checklist on the statements.
- Output: Pass / Flag / Not applicable per item + verdict.
- If verdict is Flagged on a material item, stop and resolve it before Step 3.

## Step 3 — Financial Analyst
- Ratios, DuPont, trends, cash vs profit, drivers.
- Output: initiation template, section 4 filled.

## Step 4 — Valuation Analyst (only if inputs allow)
- Method fit first. If no method fits, write that and stop — do not force a DCF.
- Inputs, build, implied test, peer check, downside, one-sentence test.
- Output: initiation template, section 5 filled, or "Valuation not built,
  because…".

## Step 5 — Reality Checker
- Number audit, claim audit, logic audit.
- Output: fixes list, line by line. Apply the fixes. Re-run this step if a
  fix changed a number.

## Done when
Thesis + financials + risks with triggers + downside + falsifier + open
questions are all present, every number is sourced, and the Reality Checker
verdict is Pass. Anything less is labelled In progress.
