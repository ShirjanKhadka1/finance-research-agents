# Nepal research → use case runbook

> Research only, not investment advice. This runbook turns completed Nepal
> research into killable applied proposals. It never turns research into a
> recommendation, a ranking presented as a verdict, or a trade.

## Team, in order
Nepal Market Researcher → Data Scout → Ledger Controller → Use-Case
Generator → Reality Checker. (For sector work, the business scorecard
rule below travels with every proposal.)

## Steps
1. **Start from completed research only.** The input is a finished Nepal
   study (company, sector, or market) with sourced numbers + periods.
   Inputs-only studies are not eligible — a proposal citing one carries
   its Inputs-only status and cannot be presented as grounded.
2. **Nepal grounding pass.** Nepal Market Researcher + Data Scout confirm:
   exchange stated as NEPSE on every ticker; fiscal calendar (BS/AD)
   stated; Nepal frictions applied where costs matter, from the sourced
   schedule only (broker slabs 0.36/0.33/0.31/0.27/0.24% min Rs 10,
   SEBON 0.015%, DP Rs 25, CGT 5%/3.75%, circuit ±15%, T+2 with broker-58
   T+1 override); intraday described as UPCOMING, never live; data
   CSV-only, no invented live feed.
3. **Ledger freeze.** Ledger Controller certifies the research inputs
   (control totals, cut-off, restatement log) before any proposal is
   generated from them.
4. **Generate, at most five.** Use-Case Generator writes proposals in its
   fixed shape: evidenced problem, named beneficiary, proposed study or
   build, data needed with licence/terms or licence UNKNOWN, feasibility
   verdict, and a kill criterion. A proposal whose data does not exist,
   whose licence forbids the use, or whose premise is unevidenced is
   killed and reported as killed — killing is a successful output.
5. **Sector rule (non-negotiable).** Sector lists stay **alphabetical,
   not ranked**. A proposal may be ranked only where its evidence
   quality is ≥ 3 on the business scorecard (primary data, filings, or
   official statistics); below that, it is listed alphabetically with
   its evidence score shown, never as a ranked recommendation.
6. **Reality check.** Reality Checker verifies every proposal's citations,
   frictions, and kill criteria before anything is shared. A proposal
   that fails is cut or sent back — never softened.

## Done when
- Every proposal traces to a finding in completed research, with source + period.
- Killed candidates are reported alongside live ones, with reasons.
- Sectors are alphabetical unless the evidence-≥3 ranking rule is met and shown.
- No proposal recommends buying, selling, or holding anything, and none
  presents an unfinished study as a valuation.
