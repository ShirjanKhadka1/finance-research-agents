# Runbook: Earnings review

**Team, in order:** Financial Analyst → Reality Checker

1. Financial Analyst: headline vs last year vs expectation, what actually
   moved (1–2 drivers), quality check (one-offs, cash vs profit), guidance.
2. Reality Checker: number audit + thesis impact check.
3. Output: earnings-review template, with a thesis-impact paragraph:
   strengthened, weakened, or unchanged — and the single number that would
   change that answer next quarter.
